Review excerpt

This is yet another version of the gga_x_pbe functional. In this case, the parameter $\mu=0.260$ in eq:pbeenh was determined from the density-gradient expansion of the semiclassical neutral atom,291,558 which is used here as the reference system (see gga_x_eb88). Combined with its correlation counterpart gga_c_apbe, this asymptotic PBE-like functional (APBE) provides accurate results for atomization energies and geometries of various types of molecules (organic, metal complexes, and transition-metal dimers). However, APBE is less accurate than PBE (gga_x_pbe + gga_c_pbe) for solid-state properties.

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
