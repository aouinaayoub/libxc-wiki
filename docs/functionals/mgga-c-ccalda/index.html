Review excerpt

This correlation functional was designed with the goal of providing a good accuracy for the atomization energies of molecules when combined with mgga_x_task. The functional mgga_x_task has a reduced self-interaction error and provides the exact energy of the H atom, which suggested Lebeda et al.757 to combine it with mgga_c_cc that shares similar features. This combination leads to atomization energies that are in most cases improved compared to those obtained with mgga_x_task + lda_c_pw. Also, the good accuracy of mgga_x_task for the band gap of solids is not altered by the addition of mgga_c_cc.
