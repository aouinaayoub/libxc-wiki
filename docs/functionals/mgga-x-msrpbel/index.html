mgga_x_msrpbel

meta-gga exchange

Review excerpt

These three functionals have the same construction as mgga_x_ms0: the global $\tau$-MGGA enhancement factor is given by eq:fx-ms0 , where $F_\text{x}^1(s_\sigma)$ and $F_\text{x}^0(s_\sigma)$ are GGA-type enhancement factors, while $f(\alpha_\sigma)$ ( eq:ms0f with $b=1$) is the $\tau$-MGGA component of the functional. After choosing a form for $F_\text{x}^1(s_\sigma)$, $F_\text{x}^0(s_\sigma)$ is then obtained by just replacing $\mu s_\sigma^2$ by $\mu s_\sigma^2+c$, where $c$ is a parameter to be determined.

The functions $F_\text{x}^1(s_\sigma)$ used in mgga_x_msb86bl, mgga_x_mspbel, and mgga_x_msrpbel are eq:b86mgc of gga_x_b86_mgc with redefined constants,

eq:pbeenh of gga_x_pbe, and eq:RPBE of gga_x_rpbe, respectively. The values of $\mu$ in these enhancement factors differ from the original values and are here chosen to be $\mu=\mu^{\text{GE}}$ (more appropriate for metals), while $\kappa=\kappa^{\text{PBE}}$ was deduced from the LO bound. The parameter $c$ in the corresponding $F_\text{x}^0(s_\sigma)$ was determined such that the exact exchange energy of the H atom is reproduced, and the obtained values are $c=0.08809161$ (mgga_x_msb86bl), $c=0.1036199$ (mgga_x_mspbel), and $c=0.0767086$ (mgga_x_msrpbel). gga_c_regtpss has been used for correlation.

By construction, these functionals should describe both metals (when $\alpha_\sigma\approx 1$) and single-orbital regions (when $\alpha_\sigma\approx 0$) well. This is argued to be the reason for their accurate description of the dissociative chemisorption of \ce{H2} on metals, as well as of the lattice constants of bulk metals.

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
