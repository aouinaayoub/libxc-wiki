Review excerpt

Starting from the density-matrix expansion, Tsuneda and Hirao691 derived a functional that has a rather simple analytical form and has no adjustable parameter. The enhancement factor is given by

They showed that this functional satisfies several conditions obeyed by the exact functional, like the LO bound, eq:lieboxford3 , or possibly the coefficient in the density-gradient expansion in the limit of a slowly varying density. The functional, that was named HF-$\tau_\sigma$ in 691, leads to exchange energies for the atoms H to Ar that are too large (i.e., too negative) by 10–20\%.
