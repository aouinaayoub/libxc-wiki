Review excerpt

Peverati and Truhlar713 proposed to extend gga_x_n12 by adding a dependence on the kinetic-energy density via $w_\sigma$ ( eq:b00w ):

where $\tilde{v}_\sigma$ and $\tilde{u}_\sigma=\tilde{u}(x_\sigma)$ are given by eq:v with $\omega_{\sigma}=2.5$, and eq:ubecke with $\gamma_{\sigma}=0.004$, respectively. Akin to gga_x_n12, this functional is not a pure exchange functional, but rather a nonseparable xc functional. The parameters $a_{ijk}$ in eq:mn12-l were optimized together with those of mgga_c_mn12_l using molecular and solid-state databases. The only constraint that was applied during the optimization was an upper bound of 25 for the absolute values of $a_{ijk}$. Compared to N12 (gga_x_n12 + gga_c_n12), MN12-L improves the results for most of the tested molecular and solid-state properties.

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
