Review excerpt

The starting point of this functional is the second-order Møller–Plesset perturbative expansion of the correlation energy for the HEG as a power series in $r_s^{-1}$ at the low-density limit. Of course, the resulting expression does not reproduce the correct divergence at high density, where lda_c_rpa is exact. where $a$, $b$, and $c$ are to be determined. He set $b=c$ based on arguments about the connection between the low- and high-density regimes. Dependence on spin is included with eq:ldavBHsi , and the following values for $a$ and $b$ are deduced from the RPA high-density limit:268,274 $a^\text{P}=\left[\ln(2)-1\right]/\left(2\pi^2\right)$, $b^\text{P}=20.4562557$, $a^\text{F}=a^\text{P}/2$, and $b^\text{F}=27.4203609$.
