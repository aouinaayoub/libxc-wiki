Review excerpt

These three functionals were proposed as alternatives to gga_x_pbe_r for the exchange part of the vdW-DF functional for van der Waals interactions.505,506 All three contain empirical parameters that were tuned in order to minimize the mean absolute error on the intermolecular interaction energies of the S22 dataset of molecular dimers.550

The gga_x_pbek1_vdw functional has the same form as gga_x_pbe and the optimization led to $\kappa=1.00$ ($\mu=\mu^{\text{PBE}}$ was kept fixed). Note that this value of $\kappa$ does not ensure the fulfillment of the LO bound.

Next, gga_x_optpbe_vdw consists of a mixture of gga_x_pbe and gga_x_rpbe:

where the mixing fraction $a$, as well as $\kappa$ and $\mu$ in eq:pbeenh ; eq:RPBE , were optimized, leading to $a=0.945268$, $\kappa=1.04804$, and $\mu=0.175519$.

Finally, gga_x_optb88_vdw has the same form as gga_x_b88, and the optimization led to $\beta = 0.22 C_\text{x}C_s^2\approx 0.003369$501 and $\gamma = 1/\left(1.2C_\text{x}C_s\right) \approx 6.981317$501 for the parameters in eq:fxb88 . Note that this value of $\gamma$ is incompatible with the exact asymptotic behavior of the exchange energy density that was used in constructing gga_x_b88.

The vdW-DF variant using gga_x_optb88_vdw for exchange is clearly more accurate than gga_x_pbek1_vdw or gga_x_optpbe_vdw for the S22 dataset.

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
