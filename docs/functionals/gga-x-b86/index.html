In order to resolve the divergence problem in the vacuum caused by a term proportional to $x_\sigma^2$ in the enhancement factor $F_\text{x}$,483 Becke172 proposed

This form conveniently cuts off the exchange energy-density in the regions where the reduced-density gradient $x_\sigma$ becomes too large. The value $\alpha=2/3$ was chosen to recover lda_x for constant densities, while $\beta=0.0036$ and $\gamma=0.004$ were fit to the HF exchange energy of 20 atoms from H to Xe. Becke172 also noted that the optimal value of $\beta$ is virtually independent of the atom.

Note that eq:fxb86 has in fact the same analytical form given by eq:pbeenh of gga_x_pbe, and the corresponding parameters for gga_x_b86 are $\mu^{\text{B86}}=\beta/\left(C_s^2C_{\text{x}}\right)=0.235$ and $\kappa^{\text{B86}}=\mu^{\text{B86}}C_s^2/\gamma=0.967$.
