Stewart and Gill412 tried to develop a functional that retains the accuracy of gga_c_lyp with a simpler analytical formula that is more physically transparent. With that in mind, they decided to retain the first term of the gga_c_lyp correlation energy, leading to

where $a$ and $b$ are parameters, and OW stands for optimized Wigner. This is thus a generalization of lda_c_wigner ( eq:ldacwigner ) to the spin-polarized case. Two versions of this functional were given: (i) lda_c_ow_lyp, where $a=0.04918b$ and $b=\left[3/\left(4\pi\right)\right]^{1/3}/0.349$ were fixed to their values in the gga_c_lyp functional, and (ii) lda_c_ow, with $b$ unchanged, but $a=0.0526b$ optimized to reproduce the correlation energy of the ten lightest atoms (H to Ne). Tests of their correlation functionals on the G2 dataset430,431 in combination with gga_x_b88 led to the finding that lda_c_ow_lyp gives overall similar results to the more complicated gga_c_lyp.
