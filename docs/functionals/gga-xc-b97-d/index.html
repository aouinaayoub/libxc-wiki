Review excerpt

This GGA functional again has the flexible B97 mathematical form389 (the review). It was designed to be combined with Grimme's D2 empirical dispersion correction for noncovalent interactions.544 The truncation in eq:b97g ; eq:fxb97 was chosen as $m=2$ as in B97 to avoid overfitting. The parameters $c_{i\,\sigma\sigma'}$ and $c_{i\sigma}$ were fit together with the parameters of the D2 correction to a training set composed of 30 atomization energies of molecules, 8 ionization energies of atoms, 3 proton affinities, 15 chemical reactions, and 21 noncovalently bound complexes. This functional, combined with the D2 dispersion correction, leads to high-quality structures and interaction energies of van der Waals complexes, as well as various properties of covalently bound systems.

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
