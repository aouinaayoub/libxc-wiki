Review excerpt

The enhancement factor of this functional is defined by a power series

where $\tilde{u}_\sigma=\tilde{u}(x_\sigma)$ given by eq:ubecke that was originally used by Becke172 in his functional gga_x_b86, and $\tilde{v}_\sigma$ is eq:v . This form was inspired by the expansions proposed in earlier works.389,565 It is important to note that $F_\text{xc}^\text{N12}$ not only depends on the reduced-density gradient $x_\sigma$ (or $s_\sigma$) as a GGA exchange functional usually does, but it also depends on the density $n_\sigma$ (i.e., $F_\text{xc}^\text{N12}=F_\text{xc}^\text{N12}(n_\sigma,x_\sigma)$).

The expansion was truncated at $m=m'=3$ (16 linear parameters), which was concluded as large enough for good accuracy without leading to numerical instability. The value $\gamma_{\sigma}=0.004$ for the parameter in eq:ubecke is originally from gga_x_b86, while $\omega_{\sigma}=2.5$ in eq:v was determined in 170. The coefficients $a_{00}$, $a_{10}$, $a_{20}$, and $a_{30}$ were obtained analytically from the expansion to third order in $\tilde{u}_\sigma$ of gga_x_pbe_sol. As such, in the low-density limit eq:fxcn12 reduces to gga_x_pbe_sol and to lda_x if $x_\sigma=0$. The remaining parameters were fit together with the parameters of the correlation part gga_c_n12 to a series of databases including both energetic and structural data of molecules and solids.

N12 (gga_x_n12 + gga_c_n12) was then tested on a few properties or types of systems (e.g., semiconductors band gaps or cohesive energies of solids) that were not used in the training set. The results were quite accurate except for the band gaps.

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
