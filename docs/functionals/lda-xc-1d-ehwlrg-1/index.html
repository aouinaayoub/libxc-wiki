Review excerpt

LDA functionals are usually derived from the HEG as the reference system. Entwistle et al.481 used alternative systems, namely few-electron slabs that resemble the HEG in a finite region of space. To illustrate this concept they decided to work in 1D, using a soft-Coulomb interaction of the form $v(x)=1/\left(\vert x \vert + 1\right)$ (compare to eq:softCoulomb ). The exact xc energy was then calculated for slabs containing one, two, and three electrons, and fit to the form

The fit parameters turn out to be $\alpha = 0.638$, $a_1=-0.803$, $a_2=0.82$, and $a_3=-0.47$ for one-electron slabs (lda_xc_1d_ehwlrg_1), $\alpha = 0.604$, $a_1=-0.74$, $a_2=0.68$, and $a_3=-0.38$ for two-electron slabs (lda_xc_1d_ehwlrg_2), and $\alpha = 0.61$, $a_1=-0.77$, $a_2=0.79$, and $a_3=-0.48$ for three-electron slabs (lda_xc_1d_ehwlrg_3). The application of these functionals on various model systems (e.g., harmonic well) shows that they have a reduced amount of self-interaction error.

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
