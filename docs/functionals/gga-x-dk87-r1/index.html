Review excerpt

Inspired by the gga_x_b86 functional, DePristo and Kress485 proposed the use of a rational expression for the enhancement factor:

where $\beta_g = 7/[432\pi(6\pi^2)^{1/3}]$ was chosen to ensure the low density-gradient limit derived by Sham163 (i.e., $\mu^{\text{Sham}}$, see the review, which was later recognized as incorrect). Furthermore, this form is proportional to $|\nabla n_\sigma|$ for large gradient ($x_\sigma\to\infty$), which DePristo and Kress485 considered as appropriate. The parameters in eq:fdk87 were fit to the HF exchange energies of the atoms He, Ne, Ar, and Kr. There are two versions of this functional, defined by $(a_1,b_1,\alpha)=(0.861504,0.044286,1)$ for gga_x_dk87_r1, where $\alpha=1$ was fixed, and $(0.861213,0.042076,0.98)$ for gga_x_dk87_r2.

Note that the spin index $\sigma$ is probably missing in the definition of $y$ given by Eq. (4) of DePristo and Kress485, since this should be $x_{\sigma}$ as defined in our work.
