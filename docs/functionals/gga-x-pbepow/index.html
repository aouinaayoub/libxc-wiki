gga_x_pbepow

gga exchange

Review excerpt

This functional takes further the ideas of power series used in previous functionals.389 Here, a power series truncated at order $m$ of the gga_x_pbe exchange enhancement factor is used:

In order for this expression to satisfy the small density-gradient behavior and the LO bound, the parameters are set to $\gamma_\text{x} = m \mu^\text{PBE} / \kappa^\text{PBE}$ and $c_{\text{x}i}=\kappa^\text{PBE}/m$. With these requirements, the functional has the same slowly (small $s_\sigma$) and rapidly (large $s_\sigma$) varying limits as gga_x_pbe. Furthermore, it is bounded for $m\to\infty$, with high-order terms ($m > 10$) not affecting significantly the results. Bremond583 recommends, however, that in practice $m$ should be chosen as large as possible, keeping in mind the computational cost. Note that the value $m=100$ is chosen in Libxc.

When combined with gga_c_pbe, gga_x_pbepow improves results for molecular covalent interactions, while keeping a performance similar to PBE (gga_x_pbe + gga_c_pbe) for noncovalent interactions.

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
