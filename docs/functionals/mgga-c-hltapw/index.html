mgga_c_hltapw

meta-gga correlation

Review excerpt

This functional has the same mathematical form as lda_c_pw, but with the density $n_{\sigma}$ replaced by $n_{\sigma}^{\text{eff}}$, given by eq:nxlta with $x=1/2$, and $n$ replaced by $n^{\text{eff}}=n_{\uparrow}^{\text{eff}}+n_{\downarrow}^{\text{eff}}$. This substitution of the density was proposed as a way to turn a LDA functional into a MLDA functional. This was used as the correlation counterpart of mgga_x_hlta.

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
