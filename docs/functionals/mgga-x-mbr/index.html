With the choice $\gamma=1$, the values $\lambda=0.877$ and $\beta=20.00$ were determined such that the exact exchange energies of the H and He atoms are recovered.

The exchange energies obtained for rare-gas atoms are close to exact and more accurate than with mgga_x_br89 and mgga_x_br89_1. Furthermore, eq:mbr-q has also been used in the context of band gap calculations of bulk solids with a mgga_x_tb09 potential modified as follows. The potential $v^\text{BR89,hole}_{\text{x}\sigma}$ in eq:tb09-v is calculated using eq:mbr-q instead of eq:br89-q . Then $\alpha=-0.030$ and $\beta=1.0$ in eq:tb09-c (not to be confused with $\beta$ in eq:mbr-f ) with $e=1/2$ were refitted. As with the original mgga_x_tb09 potential, the accuracy of mgga_x_revtb09_mbr for the band gap is good and clearly superior to that of standard functionals.
