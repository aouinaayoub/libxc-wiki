Review excerpt

In most cases, GGA functionals have been constructed using the HEG and/or atoms or molecules as reference systems in order to fix the asymptotics or to fit coefficients. These are not, however, the only possible reference systems. Vitos et al.514 used the Airy gas as a reference in their local Airy gas (LAG) functional. This is a model of an edge electron gas, previously studied by Kohn and Mattsson515, where the external potential is given as

where $-L$ is the position of the edge and $F$ defines a length scale. Vitos et al.514 chose the exchange energy of this Airy gas model to construct and parameterize a GGA exchange functional. For this purpose, they used a generalized form based on gga_x_b86_mgc

with the parameters $a_1=0.041106$, $a_2 = 2.626712$, $a_3 = 0.092070$, and $a_4 = 0.657946$. Calculations on finite and extended systems514 showed that gga_x_lag combined with lda_c_pw is in general more accurate than LDA (lda_x + lda_c_pw). Compared to PBE (gga_x_pbe + gga_c_pbe), it is worse for atoms, better for solids, and of similar accuracy for diatomic molecules.
