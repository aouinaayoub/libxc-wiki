Review excerpt

Karasiev456 noted that the simple lda_c_chachiyo functional form starts to deviate considerably from the MC data397 for large values of $r_s$. He proposed solving this problem by fitting the parameter $b$ in eq:chachiyo:log to the MC data, while keeping the value of $c$ at its original value $c=b$, with $b$ from lda_c_chachiyo. The new values are $b^\text{P}=21.7392245$ and $b^\text{F}=28.3559732$. This new parametrization leads to a much better agreement with accurate correlation energy in the low-density region, without deteriorating the quality of the functional in the mid- and high-density regions.

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
