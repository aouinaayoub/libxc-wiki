The constant $D$ is not known from the density-gradient expansion, and was therefore fit in order to minimize the error in the atomization energies of 20 molecules,165,166 leading to $D=0.113$. Finally, $\kappa=\kappa^{\text{PBE}}$ in eq:pkzbf0 ; eq:pbeenh is the same value as in gga_x_pbe.
