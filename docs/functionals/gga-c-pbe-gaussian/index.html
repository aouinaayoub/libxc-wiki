Review excerpt

The correlation component of the famous PBE (gga_x_pbe + gga_c_pbe) functional was designed to obey three exact mathematical conditions. (i) Recovery of the exact second-order density-gradient expansion in the slowly varying density limit ($t\to0$). (ii) Vanishing correlation energy in the rapidly varying density limit ($t\to\infty$). (iii) Correct scaling (as a constant) under an uniform scaling in the high-density limit ($r_s\to0$). Based on these conditions, the following ansatz for the correlation energy was proposed:

where $e_\text{c}^\text{PW-LDA}$ is lda_c_pw and $H_0$ is eq:ggac:pw91h0 , wherein $\beta=\beta^{\text{MB}}$, which is the high-density limit of the coefficient of the density-gradient expansion, condition (i), and $\gamma=[1 - \ln(2)]/\pi^2$, leading to the canceling of the logarithmic divergence of lda_c_pw, as implied by condition (iii). Besides the use of a different value for $\gamma$, the main difference compared to eq:pw91ec of the gga_c_pw91 functional concerns the term $H_1$ that is not present in eq:ecpbe , leading to a simpler correlation functional.

Note that two implementations of PBE correlation are available in Libxc. They differ in the value of $\beta^\text{PBE}$: • gga_c_pbe: $\beta^{\text{MB}}=0.06672455060314922$, which is related to $\mu^\text{PBE}=$ \\ 0.2195149727645171 (used in gga_x_pbe) via $\mu^\text{PBE} = \beta^{\text{MB}}\pi^2/3$. • gga_c_pbe_gaussian: $\beta^{\text{MB}}=15.75592 \times C_\text{c}(0) = 0.0667263212$, where $C_\text{c}(r_s=0)=0.004235$, see eq:ggacp86:rgc . Along with $\kappa^{\text{PBE}}$ and $\mu^{\text{PBE}}$ used in gga_x_pbe_gaussian, these are the values used in the GAUSSIAN program.9,139
