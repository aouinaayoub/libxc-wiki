These conditions guarantee (i) the recovery of the small density-gradient expansion at fourth order 285 ( eq:lim1-ft98 ; eq:lim2-ft98 ), (ii) a $-1/r$-type asymptotic behavior for the exchange energy density and potential ( eq:lim3-ft98 ), and (iii) a finite value of the exchange energy density close to the nuclei ( eq:lim4-ft98 ).

In eq:f-ft98 , $a\approx0.00528014$633 and $b\approx0.00003904539$633 were determined such that the coefficients in the small density-gradient expansion at fourth order of eq:f-ft98 have the correct values.285 The $a_i$ and $b_i$ parameters in eq:f1-ft98 ; eq:f2-ft98 ; eq:q2-ft98 were obtained by fitting to the exact exchange energy of a set of atoms (H, He, Be, Ne, Mg, Ar, Ca, Kr, Sr, and Xe), leading to $a_1=2.816049$, $a_2=0.879058$, $b_1=0.398773$, and $b_2=66.364138$.

When combined with mgga_c_ft98 for correlation, the functional has an accuracy similar to that of the B3LYP hybrid functional423 for atomization energies, geometries, and dipole moments of molecules.
