Review excerpt

The two parameters of gga_c_pbe were optimized as described for gga_x_xpbe, leading to $\beta=0.089809$ and $\alpha=0.197363$ (then $\gamma=\beta^2/\left(2\alpha\right)\approx0.020434$501).

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
