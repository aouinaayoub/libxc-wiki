Review excerpt

This functional of Brandenburg et al.674, B97-3c, is a revised version of B97-D544 (gga_xc_b97_d). It is composed of three parts: the GGA part gga_xc_b97_3c, a D3 dispersion correction,675 and a term that corrects for basis-set incompleteness.676 gga_xc_b97_3c is a reparametrization of the GGA part of the B97 hybrid functional389 (the review). The coefficients $c_{i\sigma}$ in eq:fxb97 and $c_{i\,\sigma\sigma'}$ in eq:b97g were optimized by fitting to a training set that includes various properties: 20 atomization energies, 43 chemical reactions, 27 non-covalent interaction energies, 4 total atomic energies, and 17 geometries of small molecules. It is reported that B97-3c is more accurate than the more expensive hybrid B3LYP-D3.

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
