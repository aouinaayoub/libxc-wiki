meta-gga exchange dim2

Review excerpt

This potential can be considered as the 2D version of mgga_x_rpp09, since it is also based on mgga_x_bj06 and the arguments invoked for its derivation are basically the same. Therefore, its mathematical form is similar to eq:rpp-v :

where $D_{\sigma}$ is also given by eq:D2 . Calculations on quasi-2D quantum dots showed that the shape of the mgga_x_2d_prhg07_prp10 potential is similar to that of the EXX-KLI.

Since the non-local Slater potential50,702,4 is expensive to evaluate, Pittalis et al.569 suggested to replace it with the approximation given by $v^\text{PRHG07-2D,hole}_{\text{x}\sigma}=2e^\text{PRHG07-2D}_{\text{x}\sigma}$ from the mgga_x_2d_prhg07 functional. This leads to the semi-local mgga_x_2d_prhg07_prp10 potential, as implemented in Libxc.

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
