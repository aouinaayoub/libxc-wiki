Another well-known approximation to the correlation energy of the HEG is the random phase approximation (RPA),268 which is based on the infinite resummation of the so-called bubble diagrams—the most highly divergent diagrams in each order of perturbation theory for the HEG. It reads as

Note that this is, in fact, the correct high-density limit, and it has therefore been commonly used in the construction of functionals.
