Review excerpt

gga_xc_kt1 consists of gga_x_kt1 plus lda_c_vwn for correlation. Since gga_x_kt1 was constructed to yield accurate potentials $v_{\text{xc}\,\sigma}$ without constraint on total energies, gga_xc_kt1 leads to inaccurate molecular structures and atomization energies. To circumvent this issue, and to improve these properties, a more flexible form was proposed:

where $e_\text{x}^\text{LDA}$ is eq:ldax3d (lda_x), $e_\text{c}^\text{VWN}$ is eq:vwnsi (lda_c_vwn), and $\Delta e_\text{x}^\text{KT1}=e_\text{x}^\text{KT1}-e_\text{x}^\text{LDA}$ is the gradient-correction term of gga_x_kt1 (see eq:f-kt1 ). The parameters $\alpha=1.07173$ and $\beta=0.576727$ were fit to atomization energies and ionization potentials. It turns out that gga_xc_kt2 improves molecular structures and atomization energies, while still maintaining the excellent accuracy observed with gga_xc_kt1 for the shielding constants.

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
