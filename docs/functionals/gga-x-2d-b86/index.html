These three functionals are the 2D counterparts of gga_x_b88, gga_x_b86, and gga_x_pbe, respectively. (Note that only gga_x_2d_b88 was published in 293, while the two others are unpublished.)

The famous gga_x_b88 functional of Becke174 can be generalized to finite 2D systems. The counterpart of eq:fxb88 reads

where $x_\sigma^{2\text{D}}$ is given by eq:x-sigma with $m=2$, and $C_{\text{x}}^{2\text{D}}$ is eq:cx2d . In this case, the coefficients $\gamma$ and $\beta$ cannot be determined by dimensional analysis. However, one can use the requirement that the 2D exchange-hole potential behaves as $1/r$ for densities that behave as $\E^{-a_0 r^2}$ at large $r$, 569 which implies $\gamma = 8$. The coefficient $\beta=0.007$ was obtained by fitting to the exact-exchange energy of a set of parabolic quantum dots.
