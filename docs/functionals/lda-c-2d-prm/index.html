Review excerpt

Pittalis et al.445 developed a functional for the 2D HEG, starting from an explicit derivation of the Colle–Salvetti formula 441 in 2D. They obtained an expression for the correlation energy similar to the original one of Colle and Salvetti441. Then, to simplify the formula they used an additional ad hoc Gaussian approximation for the two-body density matrix $\rho_{2} (\br_1, \br_2)$ in terms of the center of mass $\br = (\br_1 + \br_2)/2$ and relative $\br_{12} = \br_2 - \br_1$ coordinates:

where $c=\pi/\left[2\left(N-1\right)q^2\right]$, $N$ is the number of electrons ( eq:N-tot ), and $\beta=q\sqrt{n}$ with $q=2.258$ is a fitting parameter that was determined from a reference correlation energy for the singlet state of a two-electron parabolic quantum dot. where $\Phi=\beta/\left(\beta+\sqrt{\pi}/2\right)$.

Pittalis et al.445 also considered a modification of eq:ecprm (lda_c_2d_prm_mod). It consists of replacing $(\Phi-1)^2$ by $\Phi-1$ in the first term, and refitting the value for $q$ ($q^{\text{mod}}=3.9274$). lda_c_2d_prm_mod clearly improves over lda_c_2d_prm for the correlation energy of parabolic and square quantum dots.

The explicit dependence on the number of electrons $N$ makes these functionals formally not semi-local functionals (see discussion for lda_x_rae). The functional lda_c_2d_prm is implemented in Libxc with $N$ handled as a user-defined parameter, that is set to $N=2$ by default.
