Review excerpt

Mejia-Rodriguez and Trickey proposed and tested various deorbitalized versions of the $\tau$-MGGA functionals TPSS (mgga_x_tpss + mgga_c_tpss), SCAN (mgga_x_scan + mgga_c_scan), and MVS (mgga_x_mvs + gga_c_regtpss). The replacement of the kinetic-energy density $\tau_{\sigma}$ by an orbital-free expression depending on $n_\sigma$, $\nabla n_\sigma$, and $\nabla^2 n_\sigma$ was achieved using six different approximations, leading overall to 18 deorbitalized functionals. Among them, mgga_x_scanl and mgga_x_mvsl are the most interesting for accuracy, and they are described below.

SCAN-L (mgga_x_scanl + mgga_c_scanl) leads to an accuracy that is quite close to that of the parent SCAN functional for molecular and solid-state properties. However, the band gaps of solids are exceptions: they are smaller with SCAN-L, which can be explained by the xc derivative discontinuity.332 Note that mgga_x_revscanl, a deorbitalized version of mgga_x_revscan also using $\tau_{\sigma}^{\text{PC07}}$, has not been discussed in the literature, but is available in Libxc. The mgga_x_mvsl functional was tested more extensively in 742, where it was shown that it is more accurate than the parent mgga_x_mvs functional for various molecular and solid-state properties (excluding band gaps).
