gga exchange

Review excerpt

Akin to gga_x_pbek1_vdw, gga_x_optpbe_vdw, and gga_x_optb88_vdw, proposed previously by Klimes et al.551, this functional was designed to be used as the exchange part of the vdW-DF functional.505,506 It is a reparametrization of gga_x_b86_mgc, where the parameters $\beta=\mu^{\text{GE}}C_{\text{x}}C_s^2$ and $\gamma=\mu^{\text{GE}}C_s^2$ in eq:b86mgc were chosen such that the density-gradient expansion of the exchange is recovered. The resulting functional has an accuracy similar to gga_x_optb88_vdw on the S22 reference set of molecular dimers,550 and is quite accurate for the lattice constant and atomization energy of normal bulk solids. Furthermore, it has an improved asymptotic behavior by construction.

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
