Review excerpt

Zhang and Yang202 argued that although the gga_x_pbe functional was built using solely theoretical arguments, without resort to any fitting procedure, there is still some flexibility in the choice of $\kappa$. In fact, $\kappa$ is fixed by the {\it local} LO bound, which is a sufficient but not a necessary criterion to satisfy the integrated LO bound, eq:lieboxford3 . This led them to propose a new revised value of $\kappa=1.245$ in eq:pbeenh , obtained by fitting to exact exchange energies of atoms from He to Ar. This choice improves significantly over the original gga_x_pbe for atomic total energies and molecular atomization energies. This reparametrization of gga_x_pbe is commonly known as revPBE in the literature. Also note that gga_x_pbe_r was used as the GGA exchange component of the first non-local van der Waals functional vdW-DF.505,506

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
