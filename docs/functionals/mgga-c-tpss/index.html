The main differences with respect to mgga_c_pkzb are (i) the replacement of $z_\sigma$ ( eq:z-sigma ) by the non-spin resolved $z$ ( eq:z ) in eq:fpkzb2 , (ii) the $\max$ function in eq:ctpssmax that ensures that the correlation energy is negative for all densities, (iii) the second-order $d$-term in eq:tpss , and (iv) the constant $C$ that is replaced by a function $C(\zeta,\xi)$, where $\xi = |\nabla \zeta|/\left[2(3\pi^2n)^{1/3}\right]$. To determine $C(\zeta,\xi)$, Tao et al.183 used the fact that in the strong-interaction limit $e_\text{xc}$ should become independent of the spin-polarization $\zeta$.
