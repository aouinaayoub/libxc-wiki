Review excerpt

Ortiz and Ballone398 revisited the MC results of Ceperley and Alder397 with variational and fixed-node diffusion MC methods. They considered larger systems with better statistics and also partial spin polarization, the latter point being especially important. They provided two different parametrizations for their results, one using the lda_c_pz form and another using the lda_c_pw form. Note that the values of $C$ given in Table VI of 398 for lda_c_ob_pz have the wrong sign.

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
