Review excerpt

Pacheco-Kato et al.585 proposed two functionals that aim at simplifying the gga_x_pw91 enhancement factor, while maintaining or improving its performance. These functionals are based on gga_x_pbe and gga_x_rpbe while adding a Gaussian tail to satisfy the asymptotic reduced-density gradient constraint, eq:fxasymp . The functionals read

There are three parameters defining the functionals, specifically $\mu$ in eq:pbeenh ; eq:RPBE , $\kappa$, and $\alpha$. Pacheco-Kato et al.585 chose the value of $\kappa=\kappa^{\text{PBE}}$ as used in both gga_x_pbe and gga_x_rpbe. The small-$s_\sigma$ expansion of the functional shows that

is the second-order coefficient. Several non-empirical values for $\mu_\text{x}$ were considered: $\mu^{\text{GE}}$ of the exact density-gradient expansion, $\mu^\text{PBE}$ from gga_x_pbe, $\mu^{\text{APBE}}=0.260$ from the asymptotic expansion of the semi-classical approximation used in gga_x_apbe, and $\mu^{\text{PBEmol}}=0.27583$ from gga_x_pbe_mol, which was deduced from atomic hydrogen. This leads to four versions for each of these two functionals (thus, eight exchange functionals). Finally, $\alpha$ is chosen such that the maximum of eq:lsrpbe (related to the local LO bound) is equal to the maximum value of gga_x_pw91 ($1.641$).

These eight exchange functionals were combined with three correlation functionals (gga_c_lyp, gga_c_pw91, and gga_c_pbe with $\beta=3\mu_{\text{x}}/\pi^2$), leading to 24 functionals in total, which were tested on various molecular properties.

Among these eight exchange functionals, the ones available in Libxc are the two using $\mu_\text{x}=\mu^\text{PBE}$, gga_x_lspbe and gga_x_lsrpbe.
