Review excerpt

Ragot and Cortona440 modified the procedure of Colle and Salvetti441 in order to include explicitly the contribution of the kinetic energy to the correlation energy. This was done by deducing a Gaussian approximation to the one-particle density matrix that goes beyond HF. By applying this approximation to the HEG they arrived at an expression for the correlation kinetic energy $\tau_\text{c}$. Finally, the correlation energy was obtained by standard integration of the following relation that is valid for the HEG:278,279,442
