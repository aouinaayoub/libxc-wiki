Review excerpt

This correlation energy functional, which has often been combined with gga_x_ft97_a or gga_x_ft97_b, is based on a short-range approximation for the Coulomb hole function which describes electron pairs with same spins and with opposite spins separately. This generalizes earlier works by Gritsenko et al.612,613 to inhomogeneous systems by assuming a model dependence of the effective correlation radius on the density gradient.
