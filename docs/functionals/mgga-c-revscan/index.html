Review excerpt

Since the Taylor gradient expansion of the function $g$ in mgga_c_scan ( eq:scang ; eq:scanginfinity ) has a nonzero fourth-order term, a deviation from the second-order expansion is more pronounced than in the case of gga_c_pbe. This led Mezei et al.745 to propose the following alternative:

that has no fourth-order term in the expansion. With this revised function $g$, the parameters in eq:escan were redetermined from the following conditions (with the original mgga_x_scan for exchange): (i) high-density limit of the correlation energy of the two-electron ions ($b_{1\text{c}}=0.030197$), (ii) correlation energy of the He atom ($b_{2\text{c}} = 0.06623$), and (iii) single-orbital lower bound $e^\text{revSCAN}_\text{xc}/e_{\text{x}}^{\text{LDA}} \le 1.67082$ ($e_{\text{x}}^{\text{LDA}}$ is eq:ldax3d ) for the low-density limit of the HEG ($b_{3\text{c}} = 0.16672$).

Finally, the values $c_\text{1c}=1.131$, $c_\text{2c}=1.7$, and $d_\text{c}=1.37$ for the parameters in eq:scanfalpha were obtained empirically by a fit to the atomization energy of the AE6 test set.631,632 This was done conjointly with a refitting of parameters in mgga_x_revscan.

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
