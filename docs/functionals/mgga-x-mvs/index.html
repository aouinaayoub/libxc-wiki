Review excerpt

This made very simple (MVS) $\tau$-MGGA functional was constructed in order to obey a tight LO bound,725 together with other constraints. These include the second-order density-gradient expansion for the slowly varying density and the asymptotic expansion of the exchange energy at large nuclear charge $Z$ of neutral atoms (see 291 and gga_x_eb88). The enhancement factor is written as

In $F_\text{x}^\text{MVS}$, $k_0=0.174$ was determined from the tight LO bound at $\alpha_\sigma=0$. The denominator was designed such that eq:fxasymp is obeyed, a required condition to get a finite exchange-energy density for the nonuniform scaling limit of a density.725,567 The parameter $b=0.0233$ was fixed such that the exact exchange energy of the H atom is reproduced. Finally,

interpolates between the two regimes $\alpha_\sigma=0$ (one or two-electron systems) and $\alpha_\sigma=1$ (HEG), and tends to a constant at $\alpha_\sigma\to\infty$. The parameter $e_1 = -1.6665$ is fixed by the large-$Z$ asymptotic expansion mentioned above, and $c_1=0.7438$ so that the second-order density-gradient expansion is recovered.

Note that the enhancement factor given by eq:fmvs is rather different from most other GGA and MGGA functionals, as it decreases monotonically with increasing $s_\sigma$ or $\alpha_\sigma$. Other functionals that show a similar behavior are mgga_x_mbeef and mgga_x_scan.

Combined with gga_c_regtpss, this functional is accurate for intermolecular interaction energies (even though no dispersion van der Waals term is included) and lattice constants of solids, quite accurate for barrier heights, but rather poor for the heats of formation of molecules, similarly to PBE (gga_x_pbe + gga_c_pbe).
