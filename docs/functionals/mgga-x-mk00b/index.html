Review excerpt

Manby and Knowles685 re-derived the mgga_x_gp86 functional by using a simple Gaussian ansatz for the one-particle density matrix, which was chosen such that it fulfills four exact conditions. Tests on atoms and molecules showed that while mgga_x_gp86 is more accurate than lda_x for total energies, it is still much less accurate than gga_x_b88. However, the gap between the highest occupied and lowest unoccupied orbitals is described much more accurately with mgga_x_gp86 compared to lda_x and gga_x_b88.
