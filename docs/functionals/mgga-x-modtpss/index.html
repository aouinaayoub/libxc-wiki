mgga_x_modtpss

meta-gga exchange

Review excerpt

This is a one-parameter empirical reoptimization of the mgga_x_tpss functional. The parameter chosen to be optimized was $\mu^\text{PBE}$ in eq:f0TPSS1 , which determines the large $s_\sigma$ limit of the functional. As eq:f0TPSS1 was built to reproduce the fourth-order gradient expansion of 285, changing $\mu^\text{PBE}$ should not influence the small $s_\sigma$ limit. The values of $c$ and $e$ were also adjusted such that the constraints obeyed by mgga_x_tpss are also fulfilled by the reoptimized version. As training sets, Perdew et al.701 used AE6 (atomization energies of six molecules) and BH6 (six reaction-barrier heights).631,632

The optimized parameters are $\mu^\text{modTPSS}=0.252$, $c=1.38496$, and $e=1.37$. The value of $\mu^\text{modTPSS}$ is slightly larger than original $\mu^\text{PBE}$, but the modification yields considerably improved atomization energies and slightly improved reaction barriers.

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
