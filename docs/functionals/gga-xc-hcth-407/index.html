gga exchange correlation

Review excerpt

This is the fourth member of the HCTH family of functionals, which already included gga_xc_hcth_93, gga_xc_hcth_120, and gga_xc_hcth_147. The functional form of gga_xc_hcth_407 is the one used originally for B97389 (the review), but with $m=4$ in eq:fxb97 ; eq:b97g . For this functional, Boese and Handy658 used a considerably larger training set of 407 molecular systems, including most of the G2-2 set,659,534 to which two hydrogen-bonded dimers and eight transition-metal complexes were added. Even though gga_xc_hcth_407 was trained on many more systems, it does not systematically lead to a better accuracy compared to its predecessors.

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
