Review excerpt

By fitting new data obtained from fixed-node diffusion MC simulation of the ground state of the 2D HEG, Attaccalite et al.437 provided an analytic expression for the correlation energy as a function of the density parameter $r_s^{2\text{D}}$ (the two-dimensional Wigner-Seitz radius \(\begin{aligned} r_s^{2\text{D}} &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi n}} \end{aligned}\)) and spin-polarization $\zeta^{2\text{D}}$, defined analogously to the 3D case. For ease of notation, we imply $r_s=r_s^{2\text{D}}$ and $\zeta=\zeta^{2\text{D}}$ in the equations below. The basic idea is to interpolate between the low-density and high-density regimes.
