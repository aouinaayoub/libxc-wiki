Review excerpt

The functionals mgga_x_lta and mgga_x_tlda were derived by converting (with the help of eq:tf-inverted ) the dependence on $n_{\sigma}$ in lda_x into a dependence on $\tau_{\sigma}$. The difference between the two functionals is that the $n_{\sigma}\to\tau_{\sigma}$ conversion is done in the exchange-energy per volume in the case of mgga_x_lta, and only in the exchange-energy per particle for mgga_x_tlda. This defines a new type of functional that was termed meta-LDA (MLDA), which recovers the HEG limit lda_x. eq:fxlta has been tested on atoms (total and exchange energy) and molecules (atomization energy) for various values of the parameter $x$, and with or without correlation (e.g., mgga_c_hltapw that also uses eq:nxlta ). It was shown that choosing $x=1/2$ (mgga_x_hlta) and adding mgga_c_hltapw for correlation leads to a mean error for the atomization energy of molecules that is one third of the mean error obtained with LDA. This error is, however, still much larger than the errors from standard GGA functionals like BLYP (gga_x_b88 + gga_c_lyp) or PBE (gga_x_pbe + gga_c_pbe).

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
