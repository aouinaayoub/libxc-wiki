mgga_xc_tpsslyp1w

meta-gga exchange correlation

Review excerpt

This functional was constructed using the same strategy as for gga_xc_mpwlyp1w, gga_xc_pbe1w, and gga_xc_pbelyp1w, the only difference being that a $\tau$-MGGA (mgga_x_tpss) was used for exchange in eq:e-1w instead of a GGA. The correlation was chosen as gga_c_lyp and the value of $a_{\text{c}}$ that was optimized for water clusters is $0.74$. The functional mgga_xc_tpsslyp1w performs similarly as the three other functionals.

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
