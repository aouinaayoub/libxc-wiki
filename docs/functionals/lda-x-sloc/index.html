Finzel and Baranov406 proposed a simple local approximation for the Slater potential50 ( eq:Ux-spin ) of the form

The parameters $a=1.67$ and $b=0.3$ were determined by fitting eq:ldasloc to the Slater potential calculated using the HF wavefunctions of Clementi and Roetti407 for the Be, Ne, Mg, Ar, Ca, Zn, Kr, Sr, Pd and Xe atoms. Even though the exponent $b$ is close to the $1/3$ of the standard lda_x, the prefactor $a$ is considerably larger ($a=\left(3/\pi\right)^{1/3}\approx 0.984745$ for lda_x), leading to a much more negative potential. Band gaps calculated with this functional are considerably improved when compared to the original lda_x.406,408,409 Note that no correlation was added to exchange in the calculations.
