Review excerpt

This is probably the most famous GGA correlation functional in the quantum chemistry community. It was derived from the ($\nabla^2 n$,$\tau$)-MGGA functional mgga_c_cs of Colle and Salvetti441 by using a second-order density-gradient expansion for the kinetic-energy density $\tau$.604 The resulting functional has a dependence on $\nabla^2 n$ that is then eliminated by a suitable integration by parts.605 The functional form reads

BLYP (gga_x_b88 + gga_c_lyp) and the hybrid B3LYP423 functionals have been among the most popular GGA functionals for calculations of finite systems.
