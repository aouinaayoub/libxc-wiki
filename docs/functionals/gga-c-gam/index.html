Review excerpt

This functional is the correlation companion of gga_x_gam. It has the same analytical form as gga_c_n12, which is based on the B97 functional form389 (the review). The truncation $m=4$ was chosen in eq:b97g , and the 10 linear coefficients of the functional were fit together with those in gga_x_gam. However, contrary to gga_c_n12, the functional was not constrained to recover the LDA for the HEG.

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
