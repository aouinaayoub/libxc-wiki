Review excerpt

The gga_xc_th3 functional, whose expression is given by eq:th , was designed using the same procedure as for gga_xc_th2, i.e. by fitting near-exact (shifted) ZMP472 xc potentials, atomization energies of molecules, and xc energies of atoms, but with an extended training set, which includes 68 systems (53 molecules and 15 atoms) taken from the G1 set.651,654 The gga_xc_th3 functional outperforms gga_xc_th2, especially for systems including second-row atoms. Geometries are of a similar quality as with BLYP (gga_x_b88 + gga_c_lyp).

Handy and Tozer653 decided to propose yet another version of their functional, gga_xc_th4, by adding the error in the forces to the fitting procedure. Their objective was to improve the usual overestimation of bond lengths by most GGA functionals. The resulting gga_xc_th4 yields energetics similar to gga_xc_th3, but now yields geometries that are more accurate, and as good as the results obtained with the hybrid functional B3LYP.423

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
