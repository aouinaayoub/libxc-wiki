Review excerpt

This functional has the same analytical form as the correlation component of the M08-HX and M08-SO hybrid functionals.795 The tradition of using the Stoll ansatz (see the review) to describe separately the opposite- and parallel-spin contributions to the correlation energy is here not followed, as this had been shown to be inaccurate for the HEG.388 Consequently, the self-interaction correction factor $1-z_\sigma$ (used in mgga_c_m06_l for instance) is also not used, since it can lead to convergence problems.796 Instead, an interpolation between the lda_c_pw and gga_c_pbe functionals is used:

where $H_0$ is the gradient correction of gga_c_pbe (see eq:ecpbe ). The variable $w$ is defined by eq:b00wnospin and $m=8$ is chosen for the sums in the case of mgga_c_m11_l, while $m=11$ was chosen for M08-HX and M08-SO.795 The parameters $c_i$ and $d_i$ were fit together with the parameters in the associated exchange functional, mgga_x_m11_l. The correct HEG limit (LDA, represented by lda_c_pw) and the second-order term in the density-gradient expansion were used as constraints for the parameters.
