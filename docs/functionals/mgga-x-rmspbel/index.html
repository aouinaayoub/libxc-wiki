mgga_x_rmspbel

meta-gga exchange

Review excerpt

These functionals have the same mathematical form as mgga_x_msb86bl, mgga_x_mspbel, and mgga_x_msrpbel, but use the regularized iso-orbital indicator $\tilde{\alpha}_\sigma$ proposed for mgga_x_r2scan, given by eq:r2scanalpha with $\eta=10^{-3}$, for evaluating the switching function from eq:ms0f that is used in the enhancement factor ( eq:fx-ms0 ).

Calculations of adsorption energies and dissociation barriers of molecules on Cu(111) surfaces show that mgga_x_rmsrpbel can compete with gga_xc_beefvdw, when combined with the gga_c_regtpss and rVV10775 correlation functionals.

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
