This functional clearly improves over its predecessors gga_xc_kt1 and gga_xc_kt2 for various molecular properties (atomization energies, bond lengths, ionization potentials, etc.), while still being as accurate for the shielding constants. which has the same form as the gradient-correction term of gga_x_optx (see eq:f-optx ). eq:e-kt3 has five parameters that were determined empirically: $\gamma=-0.004$ and $\delta=0.1$ in eq:f-kt1 were determined from shielding constants, $\alpha=1.092$ was adjusted for bond lengths, while $\beta=0.864409$ and $\epsilon=-0.925452$ were fit to atomization energies.
