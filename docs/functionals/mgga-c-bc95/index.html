Review excerpt

Becke182 developed a correlation functional based on four minimal requirements: (i) correct HEG limit, (ii) separate handling of the parallel- and opposite-spin correlations (see the review), (iii) vanishing correlation energy for one-electron systems, and (iv) accurate fit to exact correlation energies of atoms. Requirement (iii) can be satisfied with the iso-orbital indicator $\alpha_\sigma$ that vanishes for any one-electron system and can therefore remove a part of the self-interaction error. However, Becke182 observed that using in eq:stollfunc the simple form $F_{\text{c}\,\sigma\sigma}=\alpha_\sigma$ for the parallel-spin correlation (with $F_{\text{c}\,\uparrow\downarrow}=1$) leads to a considerable overestimation of the correlation energy.
