Review excerpt

This variant of the gga_c_pbe functional, also called vPBEc in the literature, was proposed by Perdew et al.623 as a building block of their mgga_c_revtpss functional. It incorporates the density dependence of the parameter $\beta$ of Ma and Brueckner168 that was later derived by Hu and Langreth603 at the beyond-RPA level. The fit to the Hu and Langreth603 result reads

where $\beta_0=\beta^{\text{MB}}$, $a=0.1$, and $b=0.1778$. eq:vpbe-beta replaces the constant $\beta=\beta^{\text{MB}}$ in eq:ggac:pw91h0 ; eq:pw91a of gga_c_pbe, and was chosen such that the second-order density-gradient terms for exchange and correlation cancel in the low-density limit ($r_s\to\infty$). The use of eq:vpbe-beta leads to slightly larger atomization energies and smaller surface energies than with the standard gga_c_pbe functional. The functional gga_c_regtpss is used as the correlation component in the $\tau$-MGGA functionals MS0, MS1, and MS2,625,626 as well as MVS,627 whose exchange components are mgga_x_ms0, mgga_x_ms1, mgga_x_ms2, and mgga_x_mvs, respectively.
