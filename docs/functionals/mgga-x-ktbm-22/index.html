Review excerpt

25 functionals were trained with the goal of exploring the limit of the MGGA approximation for exchange and proposing a functional that shows a good and well-balanced accuracy for three selected properties of bulk solids (lattice constant, cohesive energy, and band gap). The form chosen for the enhancement factor is the Padé type:

where $c_{ij}$ and $d_{ij}$ are parameters that were determined by fitting experimental data for the three aforementioned properties. The training sets consists of 44 solids for the lattice constant and cohesive energy,777 and 440 solids for the band gap.409 $N=2$ in Eq. (681) was used and the correct HEG limit (lda_x) was enforced with

leading to 11 independent parameters. Using a larger value for $N$ or releasing the HEG-limit condition led to no significant improvement in the accuracy of the functional. The training of Eq. (681), combined with mgga_c_scan for correlation, was carried out by minimizing a loss function that consists of a linear combination of the mean absolute relative error of the three properties. In total, 25 choices for the relative weights of the mean absolute relative error of the three properties were considered, resulting in 25 different parametrizations of Eq. (681), all available in Libxc. Among them, mGGA23 (mgga_x_ktbm_23 in Libxc) has a quite good accuracy for all three properties. mGGA23' (mgga_x_ktbm_gap in Libxc) is a variation of mGGA23 that was trained using the mean absolute error instead of the mean absolute relative error. It is accurate for the band gap, but not for the two other properties.

Restricting the summations over $j$ in Eq. (681) to $j=0$ leads to GGA functionals (see gga_x_ktbm_a and gga_x_ktbm_b).
