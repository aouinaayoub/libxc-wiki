By a simple minimization procedure they obtained the value $\kappa = -0.047$, thus defining the gga_x_kgg99 functional. By adding this term, the atomization energy of molecules is clearly improved. At $x_\sigma=0$, $F_\text{x}^{\kappa\text{GG99}} \approx 1.0369$, which is also close to lda_x.
