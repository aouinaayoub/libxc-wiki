Review excerpt

The energy spectrum of the HEG is continuous, while that of finite systems consists of discrete states up to the ionization threshold. The idea from Krieger et al.639 for constructing the functional mgga_c_kcis is to include the discrete character of the spectrum of finite systems by considering the HEG with a gap in the excitation spectrum (measured locally by $G$, defined below). Besides recovering the correct limit of the HEG and having the proper scaling behavior in the high-density limit, the functional is also free of one-electron self-interaction error, since $z_{\sigma}=1$ for one-electron systems, leading to a cancellation of the two terms in eq:kcis .
