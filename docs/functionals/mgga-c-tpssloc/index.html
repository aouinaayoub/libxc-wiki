mgga_c_tpssloc

meta-gga correlation

Review excerpt

This functional was derived by first substituting gga_c_pbe for gga_c_pbeloc in eq:ctpsse ; eq:ctpssmax of mgga_c_tpss. The new values $d=4.5$ in eq:tpss and $c_0=0.35$ in eq:czeta0 were obtained from a fit to data of jellium surfaces636 and the Hooke atom.794

Similarly to what was observed with gga_c_pbeloc, mgga_c_tpssloc exhibits a better compatibility with HF/EXX, compared to mgga_c_tpss or mgga_c_revtpss.

Adapted from Tran, Lehtola, Pittalis, and Marques, Semi-Local Exchange-Correlation Approximations in Density Functional Theory, arXiv:2602.17333.
